# Kilifi childhood DTP serology, 2009-2021

This table transcribes age-specific values from the supplementary tables of the Kilifi, Kenya repeated serosurvey (2009, 2011, 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2021). Age strata are 1-4, 5-9 and 10-14 years, matching the model's reconstructed age groups. The 2021 rows are used in Figure 5 panel a and are compared with Kenya's 2021 programme-derived gap.

Diphtheria is reported as the percentage with anti-toxin IgG at or above the minimum protection threshold (>=0.011 IU/mL); full and long-term categories are retained for audit. Pertussis has no established serological correlate of protection. The table retains the source anti-PT categories: <20 EU/mL, 20-<62.5 EU/mL, 62.5-<125 EU/mL (operationally recent infection within about 12 months) and >=125 EU/mL (operationally recent infection within about 6 months). Figure 5 shows the sum of the last two categories as an operational recent-exposure marker, not a protection or susceptibility estimate.

Primary source: *Seroprevalence of antibodies against diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis over a 12-year period in children in Kilifi, Kenya (2009-2021)*, International Journal of Infectious Diseases (2026), doi:10.1016/j.ijid.2026.108396. Exact supplementary values were transcribed from the authors' supplementary tables (pre-publication source file: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/early/2025/10/09/2025.10.07.25337523/DC1/embed/media-1.docx).

The Kilifi sample is a single population and is not nationally representative of Kenya or a global serosurvey. It is retained as a labelled external age-profile comparison, not pooled with NHANES or European surveys.
